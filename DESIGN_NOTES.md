# Train/Test Evaluation Design Notes

Current design (iteration 11; full rationale in `HARDENING.md`):

- **Train side** (`eval/train/evaluate.py`, `scripts/evaluate_train.sh`). Public dev worlds (`data/worlds/<experiment>/dev/`), generated by `data/synthetic_activations.py`. Dictionaries at `checkpoints/methods/<m>/<experiment>/dev/crosscoder.npz` are scored on 32,768 fresh dev inputs.
- **Test side** (`eval/test/evaluate.py`, `scripts/evaluate_test.sh`). Hidden held-out worlds (`eval/test/worlds/`, secret seeds in `eval/test/build_heldout.py`). Methods only see their unlabeled training activations (`data/worlds/<experiment>/heldout/train.npz`). Dictionaries at `.../heldout/crosscoder.npz` are scored on 32,768 inputs sampled at test time.
- Both sides recompute activations, reconstructions and metrics from the submitted parameters, use the same metrics and budgets (D ≤ 256, mean L0 ≤ 8.8), share no code, enumerate methods from disk, and give failed runs the worst value of every metric.
- Frozen reference/baseline artifacts: `eval/{train,test}/reference_methods/{batchtopk,l1}/`.

The notes from earlier iterations (the `val_outputs.npz` contract with 50/50 validation and concept-family slices) describe a design that was replaced after the red-team audit (`REDTEAM.md`).
